Dr. Nithin V. Sabu

INSPIRE Faculty · Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, National Institute of Technology Calicut

Nithin V. Sabu received the B.Tech. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Sree Buddha College of Engineering, University of Kerala, in 2013, and the M.Tech. degree in Communication Systems from T.K.M. College of Engineering, University of Kerala, in 2016. He received the Ph.D. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur in 2023, specializing in molecular communication. He then served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Michigan State University, USA, during 2023–2024. He is currently an INSPIRE Faculty member at the National Institute of Technology Calicut, holding the INSPIRE Faculty Fellowship awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. His research interests include molecular communication, wireless communication, and stochastic geometry.

Research

Research interests

Molecular Communication

Principles of communication at the molecular level, focusing on biological and nanoscale systems for new methods of data transmission.

5G/6G Wireless Communication

Advanced wireless technologies that improve connectivity, speed, and efficiency in next-generation networks.

Stochastic Geometry

Modeling and analysis of spatial patterns in wireless networks and other complex systems.
