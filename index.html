About

Nithin V. Sabu is an INSPIRE Faculty member at the National Institute of Technology Calicut, working on molecular communication, wireless communication and stochastic geometry.

He received the B.Tech. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Sree Buddha College of Engineering, University of Kerala, in 2013, and the M.Tech. degree in Communication Systems from T.K.M. College of Engineering, University of Kerala, in 2016. He received the Ph.D. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur in 2023, specializing in molecular communication, and then served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Michigan State University, USA, during 2023–2024. He holds the INSPIRE Faculty Fellowship awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India.

Research

Molecular communication

Principles of communication at the molecular level, focusing on biological and nanoscale systems for new methods of data transmission.

5G/6G wireless communication

Advanced wireless technologies that improve connectivity, speed and efficiency in next-generation networks.

Stochastic geometry

Modeling and analysis of spatial patterns in wireless networks and other complex systems.
